# 1. Product overview

## One-line summary

ClinicMind uses AI agents to turn scattered patient records into a verified brief before the doctor sees the patient.

## The problem

Doctors in busy private clinics in Pakistan often work with limited time and incomplete information.

- **History is hard to reach:** Past reports and prescriptions are often on paper, and patients may bring them in a mixed pile.
- **Visits are short:** One Karachi outpatient study (2008 data) found about 6 minutes per patient, with about 40 patients in a 4-hour session.
- **Slow changes are easy to miss:** A value that drifts across several reports is hard to notice when each report is read alone.
- **Paperwork takes time:** Notes, prescriptions, instructions, letters and follow-ups add to the doctor's day.

The evidence above comes from limited and sometimes dated studies. It should be confirmed through interviews with practising doctors before any commercial use.

## Target users

| User | Need |
|---|---|
| **Medical specialist in a private clinic** (primary) | Understand a returning patient quickly and spend less time on paperwork |
| **Clinic assistant** | Upload files and resolve simple review items |
| **Patient** (indirect) | Receive clear instructions in Urdu |

## The solution

ClinicMind has three layers:

1. **Understand the record:** Agents read, match, structure and verify old documents.
2. **Reveal what matters:** Patient Story Replay, Hidden Trend Finder and the Morning Clinic Brief show the important changes.
3. **Reduce daily work:** Agents draft visit notes, prescriptions, patient instructions, letters and follow-ups for approval.

## What makes it different

| Common approach | ClinicMind |
|---|---|
| Stores records from the day a clinic adopts it | Rebuilds history from old paper files |
| A chatbot gives an answer in text | A visual replay shows the patient's story, and every fact links to its source |
| AI output may be trusted without a visible check | A separate step verifies values and sends doubtful items for review |
| Doctors must ask the right question | Agents prepare the brief before the visit |

**Limit of this research:** "No evidence found" reflects web searches and public product pages, not a full product review. Competitors may offer features that are not publicly described.

## Value

| For | Value |
|---|---|
| **Doctor** | Faster understanding of a returning patient, fewer manual searches and less routine paperwork |
| **Patient** | Clearer instructions and fewer requests to retell their history |
| **Clinic** | An old paper archive becomes searchable, with fewer repetitive tasks for staff |

These are the intended benefits. Version 1 is a demo and has not been tested for clinical outcomes or time saved.

## Portfolio value

The project demonstrates document AI, agent workflows, human review, evaluation, retrieval, speech input, 3D visualization and a complete web app, all applied to one real problem.
